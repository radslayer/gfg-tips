1099 Driver Payroll

Larry (or a Manager backing him up) enters this every pay period, tip week or not -- Deliveries $ Total and Setups $ Total get paid out every period regardless.

Days Driven feeds the tip-pool payout on a tip week (every day driven counts as 1.0 day, no matter how many hours — the half-day rule for hourly employees doesn't apply here). On a tip week, the payout is split using this period's days driven PLUS the prior (non-tip) period's days driven, since tips cover the full 4-week window -- see the recap below once you're on a tip week.

Deliveries $ Total and Setups $ Total are NOT counts — different deliveries/setups pay different amounts, so each field is the driver's own already-added-up dollar total for THIS period only (Larry adds up what each delivery/setup paid and enters the one total per driver, same as he did in the old Driver Payroll workbook). These feed the Payroll Report's Driver Payroll recap — this replaces that separate workbook.

Tips isn't entered here: it's the same tip-pool payout computed from Days Driven, so the report fills it in automatically once it runs.

Payroll Report (Owner only)

Builds the full audit-trail workbook (daily punches → weekly hours → man-days/tips → deductions → ADP Entry) for the pay period selected above -- runs every biweekly pay period, not just tip weeks. On a tip payout week it also pulls in the tip-pool numbers and combines this period's driver days with the prior period's for the tip split; on a regular payroll date, drivers still get paid Deliveries $/Setups $ for that period, just no tip distribution. Either way, every currently-pending PTO/purchase/ delivery/reimbursement request due this period is swept in.
